Research

Learned acceleration of iterative optimization with convergence guarantees
Théo Beille, Ozan Öktem
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, 2026 — journal paper in preparation
code & report

A convolutional network (deep unrolling) predicts the acceleration parameters of an iterative optimization algorithm, while a theoretical safeguard preserves its convergence guarantees. After ~10 iterations, the learned method is up to 10× more accurate than the classical algorithm on real clinical CT data (512×512).
